# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Renamed from `dissident` to TidyWin (`tidywin`).
- Public repository, licensed under GPL-3.0.
- Shortcuts through GitHub Pages: `irm androksi.github.io/tidywin/<name> | iex` for `windows`, `apps`, `chrome`, `brave` and `firefox`, plus `<name>-revert` to undo. Generated by `web/build.sh` in the `pages` workflow.
- Documentation in English.
- **Configure-Windows**: saves your answers and adds `-Check` (shortcut `windows-check`) to find and reapply what a Windows update brought back.
- **Clean-Windows** (shortcut `cleanup`): frees disk space item by item and shows how much was freed.
- **Repair-Windows** (shortcut `repair`): DISM, sfc and an online disk check, with a restore point first.
- **Backup-Apps** (shortcuts `apps-export` and `apps-import`): saves the installed apps list and reinstalls it after formatting.
- **Configure-Windows**: optional classic right-click menu on Windows 11.
- **Configure-Firefox**: removes the "Add to taskbar" and Picture-in-Picture buttons from the address bar, and asks whether to keep page translation.

## v1.0.0 — 2026-10-10

First release.

- **Configure-Windows**: telemetry, suggestions, junk apps, OneDrive, Edge, Windows Security, theme, taskbar, File Explorer, everyday tweaks and DNS with DNS over HTTPS.
- **Revert-Configure-Windows**: undoes what Configure-Windows changed.
- **Configure-Apps**: 100 apps through WinGet, with search, per-category selection and interactive install.
- **Configure-Brave / Chrome / Firefox**: privacy policies, with `-Revert`.
